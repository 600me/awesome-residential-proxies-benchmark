Sticky Session & Concurrency Loss · Updated 2026

Your proxy bill is not GB bought. It is GB burned on flows that died mid-checkout.

A residential IP that disappears halfway through a login or checkout invalidates the cf_clearance token bound to it, so every completed step has to be replayed from zero on a new IP. What is being calculated

  • expected step runs = steps ÷ (1 − drop) — with a drop probability drop per flow, a flow needs a geometric number of attempts; only the last one completes.
  • re-run loss rate = drop ÷ (1 − drop) — the share of step executions that are pure waste because the sticky IP died and the cf_clearance cookie no longer matches the new exit IP.
  • real traffic per flow = steps ÷ (1 − drop) × payload — the vendor meters every byte, including the bytes inside a flow that never finished.
  • monthly attempts = concurrency × (active minutes ÷ duration) × 30 and monthly GB = monthly attempts × real traffic per flow.
  • Per-request rotation is modelled with the same geometric form, but the hazard is the per-request challenge failure rate rather than an IP drop, because a rotating IP can never carry a clearance token to the next step.
