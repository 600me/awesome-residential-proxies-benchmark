Privacy Policy & Compliance Disclosures

Effective Date: October 2026 Jurisdiction: Delaware / US Compliance Data Officer: contact@mybskm.top

1. Data Collection & Telemetry Privacy

The Cloud & AI Infrastructure TCO Benchmark Lab is committed to stringent privacy standards. When browsing our technical benchmark reports, we do not require user account registration, collect personal identifying information (PII), or deploy intrusive tracking cookies.

Our web servers record standard HTTP request logs (including client IP address, user-agent string, timestamp, and requested URL) solely for diagnostic purposes, DDoS mitigation, and traffic capacity planning. Server logs are automatically purged on a rolling 14-day schedule.

2. Technical Benchmark Independence & Disclosures

In accordance with Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines and international advertising standards:

  • Some outbound links on this portal may contain enterprise referral parameters. If an organization purchases infrastructure services through these links, our research lab may receive a performance referral commission from the respective provider.
  • This referral relationship incurs no additional cost to the purchaser and does not influence our empirical measurement results. All latency percentiles, error rates, and unit cost formulas are calculated independently by automated testing harnesses.
  • We do not accept financial compensation in exchange for positive reviews or benchmark score manipulation.
  • We maintain zero participation in consumer sales gimmicks or unauthorized price alteration programs.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website utilizes zero third-party behavioral tracking cookies. Minimal local browser storage may be used exclusively to retain user UI preferences (such as light/dark mode selection and benchmark table filtering parameters).
