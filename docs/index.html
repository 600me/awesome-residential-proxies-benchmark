    🏛️ Executive Summary: The True Cost of Cloud Infrastructure

    In modern engineering and digital expansion, software budgets are routinely drained not by headline subscription fees, but by hidden friction costs:

    1. Proxy Retry Tax: Data extraction pipelines burning 35% to 58% of monthly bandwidth on failed HTTP 403/503 retry loops against fortified anti-bot systems.
    2. Hyperscaler GPU Premium: AI teams paying AWS or GCP up to $9.82/hour for H100 instances plus egress fees when specialized compute networks provide identical capacity for $2.90–$4.10/hour.
    3. Cross-Border Incorporations & Banking Roadblocks: Non-resident SaaS founders paying traditional law brokers $3,500+ for Delaware/Wyoming filings only to face banking denial or Stripe payment processor freeze.
    4. Browser Fingerprint Suspension Cascades: Multi-account performance agencies losing $8,500 to $35,000 in warmed-up ad accounts and pixel history due to virtual machine hardware leaks.
