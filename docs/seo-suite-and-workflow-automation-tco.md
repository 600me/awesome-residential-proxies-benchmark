## Part 2: iPaaS Workflow Automation TCO (Zapier vs Make vs Pabbly)

### 2.1 The Task Counting Trap & Official Pricing Landscape

A common misunderstanding in iPaaS architecture is the assumption that Zapier charges for built-in utility steps like `Filter` or `Formatter`. **To be technically accurate: Zapier does NOT count internal Filter, Formatter, or Paths steps as tasks.** Task usage is strictly measured upon the successful execution of an outbound Action Step.

However, the primary financial challenge with Zapier is its steep task volume tier inflation:
- **Zapier Professional Tier (2026 Official)**:
  - 750 tasks: $19.99/mo (annual) / $29.99/mo (monthly)
  - 2,000 tasks: $49.00/mo (annual) / $73.50/mo (monthly)
  - 100,000 tasks: $489.00/mo (annual) / $733.50/mo (monthly)
  - **300,000 tasks: $1,069.00/mo billed annually ($12,828.00/year) or $1,599.00/month on monthly billing ($19,188.00/year)**.

### 2.2 Alternative Architecture: Make.com Visual Scenarios

Make.com (formerly Integromat) structures pricing on operational credits with advanced visual branching logic and nested arrays:
- **Make.com Pricing (2026 Official at 300,000 ops)**:
  - **Core Tier**: **$182.16/month billed annually ($2,185.92/year)** or $214.31/month on monthly billing.
  - **Pro Tier**: **$268.54/month billed annually ($3,222.48/year)** or $315.93/month on monthly billing.
- **Cost Differential**: Compared to Zapier Professional at 300k tasks ($12,828.00/yr), Make Core ($2,185.92/yr) delivers a **net 82.9% savings ($10,642.08/year saved)**.

For webhook-intensive event pipelines, Pabbly Connect provides non-expiring task structures and flat-rate unlimited execution models:
